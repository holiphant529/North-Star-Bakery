Our Story

North Star Bakery started with a love for simple recipes, fresh ingredients, and the kind of baked goods that make a place feel like home. We wanted to create a neighborhood bakery where people could stop in for a morning pastry, pick up a fresh loaf of bread for dinner, or order something special for a celebration.

Made Fresh • Made With Care • Made for Our Community

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe the best baked goods start with good ingredients. Whenever possible, we choose quality ingredients and work with local suppliers. Everything we make is focused on fresh flavor, simple reciepes, and the small details that make homemade food feel special.

Meet Our Team

We are a small team that loves being a part of the community. Every morning starts early with fresh breads, pastries, and cakes going into the oven. Whether someone stops in every week or is visiting for the first time, we want North Star Bakery to feel warm, welcoming, and familiar.
