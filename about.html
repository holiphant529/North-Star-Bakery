Our Story

North Star Bakery was born from a love of doing things the traditional way. In a world that hurries, we choose the slow rise, the hand-kneaded dough, and the patience it takes to get the crust just right.

If you ever catch a glimpse of our recipe book, you'll find flour-dusted pages, handwritten notes in the margins, and secrets passed down through generations.

Born from a love of baking, traditional techniques, and connecting with our community, North Star Bakery is the place to slow down, indulge a little, and treat yourself to something fresh from the oven.

Made Fresh • Made With Care • Made for Our Community

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Freshness is the yeast we can do!

At North Star Bakery, we believe a great pastry starts long before it reaches the oven. It starts with good ingredients, good people, and a little bit of love.

Whenever we can, we source fresh eggs, dairy, and other ingredients from local and family-owned farms. We keep our recipes simple, use unbleached stone-ground flours, and skip the unnecessary stuff.

Basically, if Grandma wouldn't recognize it, we probably don't need it.

Meet Our Team

We are a small team with a passion for our community, good food, and probably way too much butter. We believe the best conversations happen over a warm pastry and a hot cup of coffee.

Our mornings start before sunrise, with dough on the counter, ovens warming up, and everything made from scratch. By the time you walk through our doors, the kitchen is already full of fresh bread, pastries, and the cozy smells of grandmas kitchen.

When you step through our doors, you aren't just a customer - you're part of the North Star Bakery family. So pull up a chair, grab something sweet, and stay awhile.
